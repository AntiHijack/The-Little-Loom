We believe that small things can create big happiness. Our aim is to turn creativity into something special, offer affordable and unique products, and make every customer feel happy with something made or selected with love.

Care & Practicalities

Frequently Asked Questions

How to care for hand-crocheted goods, candle burning tips, and custom gifting orders.

For crochet keychains, coasters, and mug sleeves, gentle spot-cleaning or hand washing in cool water with mild baby or wool soap is ideal. Gently press water out between clean towels without wringing, then reshape and lay flat in the shade to dry. For crochet flowers, simply dust with a soft clean paintbrush or a hairdryer on its coolest, lowest setting.

Always trim your wick to 1/4 inch before each lighting. On the first burn of amber container candles, allow the melt pool to reach all outer edges (about 2 hours) to avoid tunneling. For sculptural pillar and bubble candles, always burn them on a heat-safe ceramic dish away from drafts.
